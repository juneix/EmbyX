About

EmbyX

v2.0

Collect the World, Love the Beauty.

👋 Hi, I am @Juneix. EmbyX is a web app for Emby / Jellyfin, with fnOS Video support. Gallery mode brings immersive browsing to your private photo library. 🎉

All data stays on your device. Free to use.

🧩 Usage Tips

  • 📚 Full Tutorial:Juneix Vault
  • 🖼️ The gallery scans photos only and excludes video files.
